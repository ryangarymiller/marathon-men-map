## Typography
- Em dash with no spaces (word— word) for breaks and restarts; a turn cut off ends with "—".
- Numbers as the speakers say them; years as digits; "the '60s".
- Quotation marks around text read aloud.
- No "uh"/"um". Keep "like", "you know", and natural repetition.

## What is NOT a fix
- Speakers' factual mistakes (wrong dates, ages, places): leave them as said.
- Jokes, slang, profanity, bits: leave them.
- Rewording for style: never.
